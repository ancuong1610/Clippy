# Privacy Policy — Viral Stream Highlight Poster

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

Viral Stream Highlight Poster is a personal tool that the owner of the **@viral_streamhighlight** accounts uses to
publish their own short highlight videos to their own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

**What we access.** Only the owner's own account, after the owner signs in and approves access: basic profile
information (name, avatar, account id), the list of videos the account has posted and their public statistics,
and permission to upload and publish videos.

**What we store.** Access tokens are held by our authentication provider (Composio) and used only to publish the
owner's videos and read their statistics. We do not collect, sell or share data about any other TikTok user.

**Deleting data.** The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
On request we delete stored tokens and statistics.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/ancuong1610/Clippy/issues
